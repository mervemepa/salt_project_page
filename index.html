Hi, I'm Merve! [?]

I work with systems of memory, collective gestures, and quiet technologies.

This page introduces Meshwork—a project I’ve been working on throughout 2024–2025 that explores how technologies and environments intertwine. Using thread and trace as guiding metaphors, it revisits the histories of weaving and computing, and follows the patterns they leave behind. The project turns these layered interactions into a narrative—part archaeology, part cartography.

Here, you will find the full archive of the process, digital resources, and experimental documentation. For more information, contact me at mervemepa@gmail.com
